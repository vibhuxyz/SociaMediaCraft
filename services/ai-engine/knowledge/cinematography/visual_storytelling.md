# Visual Storytelling Guide

Goal
"Show, Don't Tell." Communicate the narrative, backstory, and emotion purely through visual choices rather than dialogue or voiceover.

Key Techniques

1. **Motifs & Visual Rhymes:**
   - Repeating a specific color, object, or framing technique throughout the video to create a subconscious connection. 
   - *Example:* A coffee ad where the color orange appears in the sunrise, the character's mug, and finally the brand logo.

2. **Environmental Storytelling:**
   - The set dressing tells the backstory. A messy desk with overflowing ashtrays and coffee cups says "overworked" without a single word.

3. **Character Evolution via Lighting:**
   - As a character goes through an arc, the lighting changes. E.g., starting in shadow and moving into soft, warm light as they find a solution.

4. **Scale to Show Power:**
   - Moving from an Extreme Wide Shot (character is small and helpless) to a Hero Low Angle (character is powerful and in control) visually represents their journey.

Checklist for Quality Review
- [ ] Could the audience understand the basic premise of this ad if it was played with no sound?
- [ ] Are we relying too heavily on the voiceover to explain what the product does, rather than showing it in action?
