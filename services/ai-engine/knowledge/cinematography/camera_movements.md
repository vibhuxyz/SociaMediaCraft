# Camera Movements Guide

Goal
Define how the camera moves through space to create energy, reveal information, or build tension.

Key Movements & Prompting Tokens

1. **Static / Locked-Off:**
   - *Effect:* Calm, objective, observational. Forces the audience to look at the composition or the performance.
   - *Prompt:* "Static shot," "tripod shot," "locked-off camera."

2. **Pan / Tilt:**
   - *Effect:* Moving the camera on a fixed axis (left/right for pan, up/down for tilt). Used to follow action or reveal new information.
   - *Prompt:* "Slow pan to the right," "tilt up to reveal the sky."

3. **Dolly / Tracking / Steadicam:**
   - *Effect:* The entire camera moves smoothly through space. A "dolly in" creates intimacy and focus; a "dolly out" creates isolation or reveals the environment.
   - *Prompt:* "Smooth tracking shot following the subject," "slow push-in," "Steadicam floating movement."

4. **Handheld:**
   - *Effect:* Gritty, documentary-style, chaotic, or highly energetic. Adds a sense of realism or unease.
   - *Prompt:* "Handheld camera motion," "shaky cam," "documentary style movement."

5. **Crane / Drone:**
   - *Effect:* Sweeping, majestic, establishing scope.
   - *Prompt:* "Sweeping drone shot," "aerial view flying over," "crane shot descending."

6. **Whip Pan / Crash Zoom:**
   - *Effect:* High-energy, comedic, or chaotic transitions.
   - *Prompt:* "Rapid snap zoom," "fast whip pan."

AI Prompting Note
AI video generators often default to a slow, floating dolly move. To combat this, explicitly declare the movement type in every prompt. If you want a static shot, emphasize it heavily (e.g., "ABSOLUTELY STATIC CAMERA, NO MOVEMENT").
