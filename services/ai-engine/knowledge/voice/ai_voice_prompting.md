# AI Voice Prompting Guide

Goal
Prompt AI Voice generation tools (ElevenLabs, PlayHT, Murf) to produce natural, emotion-driven performances rather than robotic text-to-speech.

The Prompting Strategy
AI voice models rely heavily on the context *surrounding* the words they are asked to speak.

1. **Use Emotional Descriptors in the Text:**
   - AI models read the emotion of the sentence. Instead of just writing "Buy it now," write: "(Excited and energetic) Buy it now!"
2. **Punctuation is Everything:**
   - Commas (,) force a slight pause.
   - Periods (.) force a full stop and a downward inflection.
   - Question Marks (?) force an upward inflection.
   - Ellipses (...) create natural trailing thoughts or hesitation.
3. **Phonetic Spelling:**
   - If the AI mispronounces a brand name, spell it exactly as it sounds. (e.g., Instead of "Nike", write "Nye-key").

ElevenLabs Specifics
- **Stability:** Lower stability (30-40%) makes the voice more expressive and dynamic, but risks breaking character. Higher stability (70-80%) makes it consistent but slightly more robotic.
- **Clarity + Similarity Enhancement:** Keep high (75%+) for commercial work to avoid audio artifacts.
