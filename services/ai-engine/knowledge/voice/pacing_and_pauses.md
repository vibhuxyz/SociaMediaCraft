# VO Pacing and Pauses Guide

Goal
Control the rhythm of the voiceover so it perfectly aligns with the visual edit and gives the audience time to absorb information.

Theory
Silence in a voiceover is just as important as the words. Without pauses, a commercial feels frantic and overwhelming.

Rules for Pacing
1. **The Rule of 2.5 Words per Second:**
   - A standard commercial voiceover reads at about 2.5 words per second.
   - *Math:* A 30-second commercial can fit a MAXIMUM of 75 words. Ideally, it should be closer to 50-60 words to allow for music transitions and visual breathing room.
2. **The Dramatic Pause:**
   - Always script a 1 to 2-second pause before the final tagline or brand reveal.
3. **Pacing the Edit:**
   - The VO dictates the visual cuts. If the VO lists three items rapidly ("Faster, Stronger, Smarter"), the visuals must cut rapidly to match.

Formatting the Script
- Use ellipses (...) to indicate a short pause.
- Use brackets to dictate a long pause: `[PAUSE 2s]`.
