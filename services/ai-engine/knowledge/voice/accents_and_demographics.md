# Accents and Demographics Guide

Goal
Cast a voice that the target audience subconsciously identifies with and trusts.

Theory
Voice casting is highly demographic-dependent. If the voice doesn't match the intended audience's self-image or aspirational image, the ad will fail.

Key Considerations
1. **Age:** 
   - *Young (18-25):* Upbeat, slightly vocal fry, casual phrasing. (Gen-Z fashion).
   - *Millennial (25-40):* The "Conversational" standard. Trustworthy, peer-to-peer.
   - *Mature (50+):* Authoritative, calm, gravelly, wise. (Wealth management, healthcare).

2. **Accents (English Language):**
   - *Standard American (General):* Broadest appeal, used for most national campaigns.
   - *British (RP):* Subconsciously signals high intelligence, luxury, or historical prestige (often used in American luxury car ads or high-end tech).
   - *Southern/Texan:* Signals ruggedness, authenticity, and hard work (Trucks, beer, outdoors).

Instruction for the Audio Director
Always specify Age, Gender, and Accent before selecting an AI Voice model.
