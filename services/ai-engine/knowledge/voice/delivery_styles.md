# Voice Delivery Styles Guide

Goal
Select the precise tone of voice (VO) to match the brand's persona and the script's emotional goal.

Core Delivery Styles

1. **The Conversational / "Non-Announcer":**
   - *Vibe:* Your smart, trusted friend explaining something to you over coffee.
   - *Characteristics:* Slight imperfections, natural breathing, varied pitch, not overly polished.
   - *Use Case:* 80% of modern commercials, especially tech, lifestyle, and healthcare.

2. **The Epic / Cinematic:**
   - *Vibe:* The "Movie Trailer" voice.
   - *Characteristics:* Deep resonance, slow delivery, intense gravity, often whispered or raspy.
   - *Use Case:* High-end cars, sports anthems, video game trailers.

3. **The High-Energy Retail:**
   - *Vibe:* The classic "Radio DJ" or used car salesman.
   - *Characteristics:* Extremely fast, loud, smiling through the read, punching every adjective.
   - *Use Case:* Weekend sales, fast food, local retail.

4. **The Intimate / Sensual:**
   - *Vibe:* Close-mic, whispering directly into your ear.
   - *Characteristics:* Very soft, breathy, slow pacing.
   - *Use Case:* Perfume, luxury fashion, premium chocolate.

Checklist for the Audio Director
- [ ] Does the voice style clash with the visual style? (e.g., Do not use an "Epic" voice for a "Minimalist" pastel skincare ad).
