# Music Tempo & Pacing Guide

Goal
Dictate the speed of the edit and the physical energy of the commercial through Beats Per Minute (BPM).

Tempo Categories

1. **Slow (60 - 80 BPM):**
   - *Vibe:* Romantic, sad, luxurious, deliberate.
   - *Editing Impact:* Forces the editor (or AI Video generator) to use long takes and slow-motion.
   - *Use Case:* A 60-second perfume ad where every frame is a painting.

2. **Medium / Walking Pace (90 - 110 BPM):**
   - *Vibe:* Groove, confidence, approachable.
   - *Editing Impact:* Standard pacing. Good for dialogue-heavy commercials where the music shouldn't distract.
   - *Use Case:* A tech explainer video or a fashion walk.

3. **Fast / Energetic (120 - 140 BPM):**
   - *Vibe:* Urgent, happy, driving, pop-centric.
   - *Editing Impact:* Fast cuts. The visuals must keep up with the driving beat.
   - *Use Case:* Retail sales, athletic montages, e-commerce direct-response ads.

4. **Frenetic (150+ BPM):**
   - *Vibe:* Chaotic, extreme action, anxiety.
   - *Editing Impact:* Rapid-fire montage, crash zooms.
   - *Use Case:* High-octane sports, trailer back-halves.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "slow tempo, 70 bpm, lingering notes"
- "driving beat, 128 bpm, four on the floor"
