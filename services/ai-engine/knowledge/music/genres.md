# Music Genres Guide

Goal
Select the appropriate musical genre to anchor the commercial in a specific cultural or aesthetic context.

Core Genres for Advertising

1. **Cinematic / Orchestral:**
   - *Vibe:* Epic, serious, sweeping, emotional.
   - *Use Cases:* High-end luxury (perfume, cars), emotional storytelling, brand anthems.

2. **Corporate / Tech (Chillhop/Lo-Fi):**
   - *Vibe:* Forward-thinking, clean, unobtrusive, optimistic.
   - *Use Cases:* Software demos, B2B services, explainer videos.

3. **Indie Folk / Acoustic:**
   - *Vibe:* Authentic, grounded, warm, human.
   - *Use Cases:* Coffee, sustainability campaigns, family-oriented products.

4. **Electronic / Bass / EDM:**
   - *Vibe:* High-energy, futuristic, aggressive.
   - *Use Cases:* Athletic wear, energy drinks, fast-paced fashion, modern tech.

5. **Jazz (Classic or Nu-Jazz):**
   - *Vibe:* Sophisticated, smooth, slightly unpredictable.
   - *Use Cases:* High-end dining, watches, bespoke tailoring, whiskey.

6. **Pop / Upbeat:**
   - *Vibe:* Cheerful, catchy, relatable.
   - *Use Cases:* Fast food, Gen-Z fashion, lifestyle apps, retail sales.
