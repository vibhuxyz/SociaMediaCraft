# Music Emotions Guide

Goal
Ensure the emotional arc of the music perfectly matches the emotional arc of the screenplay.

The Emotional Matrix

1. **Aspiration / Wonder:**
   - *Musical Traits:* Slowly building strings, ascending chord progressions, shimmering synths, lack of heavy percussion.

2. **Urgency / Tension:**
   - *Musical Traits:* Fast staccato strings, ticking clocks, heavy, repetitive basslines, dissonant chords.

3. **Joy / Celebration:**
   - *Musical Traits:* Major keys, upbeat tempo, brass instruments, handclaps, choir vocals.

4. **Nostalgia / Melancholy:**
   - *Musical Traits:* Minor keys, solo piano or cello, slow tempo, vinyl crackle (lo-fi texture).

5. **Confidence / Swagger:**
   - *Musical Traits:* Heavy hip-hop drum beats, distorted bass, brass stabs, pauses on the downbeat.

Application for the Music Director
Do not just pick an emotion for the entire track. A 30-second ad usually has an emotional shift (e.g., from "Problem/Tension" at 0:00 to "Solution/Joy" at 0:15). The music prompt must reflect this transition.
