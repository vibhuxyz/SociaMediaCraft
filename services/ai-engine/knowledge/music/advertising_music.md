# Advertising Music Structure Guide

Goal
Structure a music track specifically for the strict time constraints of a commercial (15s, 30s, 60s).

The "Arc" of an Ad Track

1. **The Hook (0:00 - 0:03):**
   - Commercial music cannot build slowly. It must grab attention immediately with a distinct sound or beat to stop the user from scrolling.

2. **The Bed / The Build (0:03 - 0:15):**
   - The music drops back slightly to allow the Voiceover (VO) to be heard clearly. It builds tension or establishes the groove without getting in the way of the message.

3. **The Drop / The Turn (0:15 - 0:25):**
   - The product is revealed, or the problem is solved. The music hits its climax (full instrumentation, chorus).

4. **The Button (0:25 - 0:30):**
   - A definitive, clean ending. Advertising music rarely fades out. It ends on a "button"—a final staccato chord or sound effect that plays exactly as the logo appears.

Prompting AI Audio Models
- Standard music models generate full songs. For advertising, you must explicitly prompt the structure.
- "30-second commercial track, instant hook, sparse middle section for voiceover, triumphant climax, hard stop ending."
