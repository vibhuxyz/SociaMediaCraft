# Instrumentation Guide

Goal
Select specific instruments that subconsciously communicate the brand's identity and target demographic.

Instrument Psychology

- **Acoustic Guitar / Ukulele:** 
  - *Implies:* Human, handmade, organic, accessible. 
  - *Avoid in:* Cyberpunk tech ads.

- **Grand Piano:** 
  - *Implies:* Elegance, emotion, history, premium quality.

- **Synthesizers (Analog / Vintage):** 
  - *Implies:* 1980s nostalgia, stranger things, synthwave, retro-tech.

- **Synthesizers (Modern / Digital):** 
  - *Implies:* The future, software, precision, cold logic.

- **Strings (Violin / Cello):** 
  - *Implies:* High drama, romance, prestige. (A solo cello is sad; a full string section is epic).

- **Brass (Horns / Trumpets):** 
  - *Implies:* Victory, triumph, grand scale, or (if jazzy) swagger and nightlife.

- **808 Drum Machine / Heavy Bass:** 
  - *Implies:* Youth culture, street credibility, modern fashion, club energy.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "instrumental, prominent solo cello, sweeping strings"
- "heavy 808 bass, sharp hi-hats, analog synthesizer"
- "minimalist acoustic guitar picking, soft felt piano"
