# Low-Key Lighting Guide

Goal
Create drama, mystery, tension, or intense luxury by letting the majority of the frame fall into deep shadow.

Theory
Low-key lighting relies on a high "lighting ratio" (a very bright key light and almost zero fill light). It leaves much of the subject and the background in darkness (chiaroscuro).

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Dominant. Often 70-80% of the frame is black or near-black.
- **Contrast:** Extremely high.
- **Mood:** Serious, intense, sensual, or threatening.

Ideal Use Cases
- High-end watches and jewelry (light only hitting the metallic edges)
- Luxury cars (light painting the curves of the vehicle in a dark studio)
- Thriller/Horror narratives
- Intense athletic apparel ads (e.g., Nike/Under Armour workouts in the dark)
