# Three-Point Lighting Guide

Goal
Master the foundational lighting setup used in 90% of all interviews and standard commercial setups to ensure the subject is well-lit and three-dimensional.

The Three Points
1. **Key Light:** The primary light source. Usually placed 45 degrees to the subject's side and slightly above eye level. It provides the main exposure and defines the shape of the face.
2. **Fill Light:** Placed on the opposite side of the Key light. Its job is purely to soften the shadows created by the Key. (A high fill ratio = upbeat/commercial. A low fill ratio = moody/dramatic).
3. **Backlight (Rim/Hair Light):** Placed behind the subject, pointing at the back of their head/shoulders. It creates a bright outline that separates the subject from the background.

Application in Filmmaking
- This is the absolute standard for corporate interviews, news broadcasts, and standard dialogue scenes.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "classic three-point lighting, distinct rim light, flattering key light"
- "cinematic portrait lighting, subtle fill light"

When to Break the Rule
- If the brand wants a gritty, realistic, or ultra-moody look, discard the Fill light or the Backlight entirely.
