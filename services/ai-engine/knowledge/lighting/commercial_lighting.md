# Commercial Lighting Guide

Goal
Ensure the product is the absolute hero of the shot, combining techniques to make the item look irresistible, expensive, and clear.

Key Techniques for Products
- **Product Wrap:** Using massive softboxes to create a smooth, continuous reflection across curved surfaces (essential for cars and glass bottles).
- **The "Hero" Spotlight:** A sharp, focused beam of light hitting only the product or the brand logo, drawing the eye immediately.
- **Gobos (Go-Betweens):** Placing objects in front of the light (like blinds or tree branches) to cast interesting shadow patterns on a flat background behind the product.

Ideal Use Cases
- Tabletop product videography
- E-commerce ads
- Food and beverage

Checklist for Art Director
- [ ] Is the product evenly lit so the logo is perfectly readable?
- [ ] Does the lighting style match the brand's identity? (e.g., High-key for a daily moisturizer, Low-key for an anti-aging night serum).
