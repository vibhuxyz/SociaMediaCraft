# High-Key Lighting Guide

Goal
Communicate optimism, cleanliness, energy, and approachability by eliminating harsh shadows.

Theory
High-key lighting floods the scene with light. The fill light is almost as bright as the key light, resulting in a very low lighting ratio. Historically used in comedies; now the standard for modern tech and beauty brands.

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Minimal to non-existent. Very soft if they do exist.
- **Highlights:** Bright, sometimes approaching pure white in the background.
- **Mood:** Upbeat, sterile, happy, safe, and modern.

Ideal Use Cases
- Skincare and cosmetics (flawless, shadow-free skin)
- Apple-style tech commercials (white backgrounds, bright products)
- Healthcare and pharmaceuticals
- Comedy and sitcoms
