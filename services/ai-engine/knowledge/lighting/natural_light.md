# Natural Light Guide

Goal
Utilize the sun (or moon) as the primary light source to create an authentic, organic, and grounded aesthetic.

Theory
Natural light implies authenticity. It is often used by brands that want to feel accessible, eco-friendly, or "real." However, natural light changes rapidly and can be harsh, requiring modifiers (like silks or reflectors) even if it *looks* untouched.

Types of Natural Light
- **Direct Sunlight (Hard):** Creates sharp, defined shadows. Evokes heat, summer, energy, or harsh reality. (Used in sports or beach ads).
- **Overcast (Soft):** Clouds act as a massive diffuser. Creates incredibly soft, flattering, even light with no harsh shadows. (Used in moody storytelling or car commercials).
- **Window Light:** A directional, soft light falling across a subject indoors.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "natural sunlight, organic lighting, authentic aesthetic"
- "overcast soft lighting, diffused natural light"
- "sunlight streaming through a window, dappled light"

Avoid
- "Flat lighting." If you just prompt "natural light," the AI might make it look like a boring iPhone photo. Always specify the *quality* of the natural light (e.g., "directional window light").
