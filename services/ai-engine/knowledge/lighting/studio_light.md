# Studio Light Guide

Goal
Create a highly controlled, perfect, and often stylized environment that places 100% of the focus on the subject.

Theory
Studio lighting is entirely artificial. It allows for perfect exposure, extreme color gels, and absolute consistency. It is the hallmark of high-end product photography, fashion editorials, and clean corporate messaging.

Visual Characteristics
- **Backgrounds:** Often solid colors (seamless paper), pure white (cyc wall), or pure black.
- **Catchlights:** Distinct, perfect geometric reflections in the subject's eyes (umbrellas, softboxes, or ring lights).
- **Separation:** Perfect rim lights separating the subject from the background.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "professional studio lighting, softbox, solid backdrop"
- "high-end editorial lighting, crisp catchlights"
- "neon gel lighting, dramatic studio setup"

Avoid
- Messy backgrounds. Studio lighting demands absolute cleanliness in the composition.
