# Golden Hour Lighting Guide

Goal
Evoke nostalgia, romance, warmth, and aspirational beauty using the most sought-after natural light in filmmaking.

Theory
Golden Hour occurs shortly after sunrise and shortly before sunset. The sun is low on the horizon, traveling through more of the Earth's atmosphere, which diffuses the light and shifts it into the red/orange spectrum.

Visual Characteristics
- **Color:** Warm amber, gold, and soft reds.
- **Direction:** Low angle, creating long, dramatic shadows.
- **Quality:** Soft and extremely flattering on skin tones.
- **Flares:** Highly prone to beautiful, organic lens flares if the camera points toward the sun.

Ideal Use Cases
- Automotive (cars driving into the sunset)
- Romance and weddings
- Travel and tourism
- Family and lifestyle branding
