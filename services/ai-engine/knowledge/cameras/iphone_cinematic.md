# iPhone Cinematic Guide

Goal
Replicate the high-end mobile creator aesthetic, mimicking the "Cinematic Mode" or Apple ProRes look that feels native to social media.

Visual Signature
- **Computational Bokeh:** The background blur is slightly artificial, sometimes struggling perfectly with hair or complex edges.
- **Color:** Highly optimized, vibrant, HDR look (sometimes overly bright in the mid-tones).
- **Focal Length:** Typically slightly wider (equivalent to 24mm or 26mm main lens).
- **Motion:** Very smooth stabilization, but clearly handheld or gimbal-based in a lightweight format.

Ideal Use Cases
- TikTok / Instagram Reels Ads
- User-Generated Content (UGC) campaigns
- "Behind the Scenes" style marketing
- Fast-paced influencer vlogs

Prompting Tokens (For Image/Video Generation)
- "shot on iPhone 15 Pro Max, Apple ProRes"
- "Cinematic Mode, computational depth of field, vibrant HDR"
- "social media creator style, vertical video framing, handheld vlog style"

What to Avoid
- Perfect Hollywood lighting and massive camera setups.
- Organic film grain (keep it digitally clean).
- Perfect, authentic bokeh (it should look slightly computational if pushing the aesthetic).
