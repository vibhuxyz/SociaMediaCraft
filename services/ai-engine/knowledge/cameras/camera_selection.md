# Camera Selection Guide

Goal
Help the Cinematography Director choose the right virtual camera system based on the project's requirements, budget tier, and target emotion.

Decision Matrix

| Project Type | Target Emotion | Recommended Camera | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| High-End Luxury | Sophistication, Timelessness | **ARRI Alexa** | Flattering skin tones and organic, soft highlight roll-off. |
| Automotive/Action | Adrenaline, Precision | **RED V-Raptor** | Extreme sharpness, vivid contrast, and dynamic motion. |
| Nighttime/Sci-Fi | Mystery, Modernity | **Sony VENICE** | Incredible low-light performance and clean full-frame separation. |
| Indie/Documentary | Gritty, Authentic | **Blackmagic** | Raw texture that holds heavy, stylized color grading well. |
| Social Media Ad | Relatable, Urgent | **iPhone Cinematic** | Looks native to the platform, builds immediate trust via UGC feel. |

Integration with Prompting
- The chosen camera should be explicitly passed to the Prompt Engineer.
- AI models (like Midjourney or Sora) use camera names as "Style Tokens." Simply appending "Shot on ARRI Alexa" changes the latent space toward professional film sets.

Checklist for Cinematography Director
- [ ] Does the camera choice align with the brand's identity?
- [ ] Are we prompting the AI with the correct accompanying tokens (e.g., matching the camera with the right lens and lighting style)?
