# RED V-Raptor Camera Guide

Goal
Achieve a highly detailed, sharp, and vivid image often associated with modern high-end action, music videos, and tech-forward commercials.

Visual Signature
- **Resolution:** Extreme detail, often implying 8K downsampled sharpness.
- **Color:** Very punchy, vivid color rendition, especially in reds and high-contrast environments.
- **Contrast:** Grittier, punchier contrast compared to the softer ARRI look.
- **Low Light:** Extremely clean in the shadows with aggressive modern sensors.

Ideal Use Cases
- Automotive Commercials
- Music Videos
- Fashion and Edgy Streetwear Ads
- High-Speed/Action Shots

Prompting Tokens (For Image/Video Generation)
- "shot on RED V-Raptor 8K" or "shot on RED Komodo"
- "hyper-detailed, crisp focus, punchy contrast, vivid REDcolor science"
- "ultra-high definition, cinematic action shot"

What to Avoid
- A soft, vintage, or hazy look (unless specifically filtering the RED).
- Low contrast, washed-out images.
