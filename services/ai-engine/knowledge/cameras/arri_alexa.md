# ARRI Alexa Camera Guide

Goal
Achieve the industry-standard "Hollywood" cinematic look, characterized by organic color science, incredible dynamic range, and soft highlight roll-off.

Visual Signature
- **Highlight Roll-off:** The smoothest transition from bright to clipped white (never harsh).
- **Skin Tones:** Extremely natural, flattering, and organic skin tones without looking overly digital or sharp.
- **Dynamic Range:** Captures deep details in shadows while retaining sky details.
- **Texture:** Film-like, with a subtle grain structure that feels analog despite being digital.

Ideal Use Cases
- High-end Commercials
- Luxury Brand Videos
- Emotional Storytelling
- Narrative Short Films

Prompting Tokens (For Image/Video Generation)
- "shot on ARRI Alexa 65" or "shot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF"
- "organic cinematic look, smooth highlight roll-off, natural skin tones"
- "ArriRAW, cinematic color grading, filmic texture"

What to Avoid
- Over-sharpened digital looks.
- Harsh, blown-out skies with jagged edges.
- "Video" looking frame rates (stick to 24fps equivalent motion blur).
