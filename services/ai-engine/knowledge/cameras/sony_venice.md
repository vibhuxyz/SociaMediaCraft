# Sony VENICE Camera Guide

Goal
Achieve a pristine, modern, full-frame cinematic look that excels in low-light and high-end studio setups.

Visual Signature
- **Color Science:** Clean, modern, with excellent separation of hues (often associated with high-end Netflix productions).
- **Depth of Field:** As a full-frame sensor, it provides incredibly shallow depth of field, separating the subject from the background effortlessly.
- **Dual ISO/Low Light:** Unbelievably clean in extremely dark environments.
- **Clarity:** Sharp but not clinical, offering a very "clean" window into the world.

Ideal Use Cases
- Neon-lit / Cyberpunk aesthetics
- Nighttime Commercials
- High-end Corporate Documentaries
- Dialogue-heavy cinematic scenes

Prompting Tokens (For Image/Video Generation)
- "shot on Sony VENICE 2"
- "full-frame sensor, extremely shallow depth of field, clean low-light performance"
- "cinematic neon lighting, crisp modern look"

What to Avoid
- Excessive film grain (Sony VENICE is known for being clean).
- Deep depth of field (unless explicitly stopped down).
