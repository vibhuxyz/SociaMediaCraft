# Spherical Lenses Guide

Goal
Achieve a clean, sharp, undistorted image that represents reality perfectly.

Theory
Unlike Anamorphic lenses, Spherical lenses project a standard image onto the sensor without squeezing it. They are the standard for 95% of all video content.

Visual Characteristics
- **Bokeh:** Out-of-focus highlights are rendered as perfect, creamy circles.
- **Sharpness:** Typically sharp from edge-to-edge.
- **Flares:** Circular, natural light blooms rather than horizontal streaks.
- **Distortion:** Minimal, providing an objective view of the world.

Ideal Use Cases
- High-end corporate videos
- Beauty and cosmetics (where exact detail is needed without edge distortion)
- Vertical (9:16) social media ads (spherical lenses crop perfectly)
- Television commercials

AI Prompt Tokens
- "spherical lens, perfectly circular bokeh, edge to edge sharpness"
- "clean optical glass, natural lens flare"
- "Master Prime lens look, pristine clarity"

When to Choose Over Anamorphic
If the brand requires absolute clarity, approachability, and realism (e.g., a healthcare ad or a food commercial), Spherical is the correct choice.
