# Macro Lenses Guide

Goal
Reveal extreme, microscopic detail to evoke intense sensory reactions and showcase premium quality.

Theory
Macro lenses allow the camera to focus on objects extremely close to the glass, magnifying tiny details so they fill the entire frame.

Visual Characteristics
- **Depth of Field:** Razor-thin. Often, only a millimeter of the subject is in focus while the rest melts into extreme blur.
- **Scale:** Makes tiny objects (a coffee bean, a watch gear, a drop of water) look monumental and massive.
- **Texture:** Highlights every pore, scratch, weave, and bubble.

Ideal Use Cases
- Food Advertising (condensation on a glass, salt crystals on a burger).
- High-end watches and jewelry.
- Cosmetics (smearing lipstick, macro skin pores).
- Tech products (microchips, speaker mesh).
