# Anamorphic Lenses Guide

Goal
Create the most iconic, stylized "movie" look possible, characterized by specific optical distortions.

Theory
Anamorphic lenses squeeze a wider image onto a standard sensor, which is then "de-squeezed" in post. This physical process creates unique optical artifacts that audiences instantly associate with high-budget Hollywood cinema.

Visual Characteristics
- **Aspect Ratio:** Very wide (usually 2.39:1 "Cinemascope").
- **Bokeh:** Out-of-focus highlights are rendered as tall, thin ovals rather than perfect circles.
- **Lens Flares:** Horizontal streaks of light (often blue, like in J.J. Abrams films).
- **Distortion:** The edges of the frame exhibit barrel distortion (curving slightly) and a fall-off in sharpness.

Ideal Use Cases
- High-budget automotive commercials
- Sci-fi and action
- Epic music videos
- Cinematic storytelling

AI Prompt Tokens
- "shot on anamorphic lens, 2.39:1 aspect ratio"
- "horizontal lens flare, oval bokeh, cinematic lens distortion"
- "Panavision anamorphic look"

Avoid
- Using anamorphic for standard social media (9:16 vertical) as the extreme width is wasted and heavily cropped.
- Using it for documentary or hyper-realistic corporate interviews where optical perfection is desired.
