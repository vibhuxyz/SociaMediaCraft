# Lens Selection Guide

Goal
Help the Cinematography Director choose the right optical package based on the project's requirements.

Decision Matrix

| Project Goal | Recommended Lens Type | Suggested Focal Length |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Epic Blockbuster Feel** | Anamorphic | 35mm (Anamorphic) |
| **Beauty / Fashion / Cosmetics** | Spherical (Portrait) | 85mm |
| **Intense Product Detail** | Spherical (Macro) | 100mm Macro |
| **Comedic / Unhinged / Vlog** | Spherical (Wide) | 16mm or 24mm |
| **Objective Documentary** | Spherical (Standard) | 35mm or 50mm |
| **Isolating Subject in Crowd** | Spherical (Telephoto) | 135mm or 200mm |

Integration with Prompting
- Lenses are powerful "Style Tokens" for AI generators. Just putting "85mm" immediately tells the AI to blur the background and focus on a single subject.
- Putting "12mm" tells the AI to curve the edges of the room and show the entire environment.

Checklist for Cinematography Director
- [ ] Are we using macro lenses to highlight product texture?
- [ ] If the video is 9:16 for TikTok, are we avoiding anamorphic lenses?
- [ ] Does the focal length match the psychological intent of the scene? (e.g., wide for isolation, telephoto for intimacy).
