# Portrait Lenses Guide

Goal
Present human subjects in the most flattering, engaging, and professional way possible.

Theory
The human face distorts based on focal length. Wide angles (under 35mm) stretch the nose and forehead, looking slightly comical. Telephoto lenses (85mm+) compress the features, slimming the face and pushing the background away.

The "Holy Trinity" of Portrait Lenses
1. **50mm (The Honest Portrait):** Closest to the human eye. Used for mid-shots and natural, relatable casting.
2. **85mm (The Beauty Portrait):** The gold standard for beauty and fashion. Slims the face, melts the background into creamy bokeh, and forces the viewer to focus entirely on the subject's eyes.
3. **135mm (The Editorial Portrait):** Extreme compression. Used when shooting a subject in a busy environment (like a city street) to completely isolate them from the chaos behind them.
