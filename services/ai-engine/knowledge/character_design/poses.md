# Character Poses & Body Language Guide

Goal
Use body language to establish power dynamics, energy levels, and narrative intent within the frame.

Common Body Language Archetypes
1. **Power / Dominance (The Boss):** Shoulders back, taking up space, hands on hips or steepled, chin slightly up.
2. **Submissive / Vulnerable:** Shoulders hunched, arms crossed defensively, head slightly down, taking up minimal space.
3. **Action / Kinetic:** Leaning forward, weight on the balls of the feet, mid-stride, dynamic diagonal lines in the body.
4. **Relaxed / Approachable (The Friend):** Leaning back, open posture, asymmetrical stance (e.g., resting weight on one leg).

Prompting for Posture
- Specify the action verb: "leaning against a wall," "striding confidently," "slumped in a chair."
- Describe the hands (AI struggles with hands, so hide them or give them a clear task): "hands deep in pockets," "holding a coffee cup," "typing on a laptop."
- Describe the head tilt: "looking over the shoulder," "tilting head in curiosity."

Interaction with Environment
Characters shouldn't float in space. Anchor their pose to the environment.
- *Good:* "Sitting on the edge of a mahogany desk, one foot touching the floor."
- *Bad:* "Sitting down."
