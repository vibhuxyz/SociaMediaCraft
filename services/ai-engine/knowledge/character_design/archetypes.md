# Character Archetypes Guide

Goal
Quickly establish a character's role in the story using universally understood psychological and narrative templates (based on Jungian archetypes).

The 12 Core Archetypes

1. **The Hero:** 
   - *Role:* To prove worth through courageous action.
   - *Visuals:* Strong posture, practical but iconic clothing, direct eye contact.
2. **The Magician:**
   - *Role:* To understand the universe and transform things (often tech or science brands).
   - *Visuals:* Mysterious lighting, sleek modern or deeply vintage clothing, intense focus.
3. **The Rebel (Outlaw):**
   - *Role:* To disrupt the status quo.
   - *Visuals:* Leather, denim, messy hair, unconventional angles, defiant expressions.
4. **The Explorer:**
   - *Role:* To discover and experience a better, more authentic life.
   - *Visuals:* Outdoor gear, weathered textures, looking out toward the horizon.
5. **The Sage:**
   - *Role:* To find the truth and share wisdom.
   - *Visuals:* Glasses, books, soft lighting, composed and still posture, older demographics.
6. **The Innocent:**
   - *Role:* To be happy and do things right.
   - *Visuals:* Bright colors, soft features, wide eyes, natural lighting.
7. **The Creator:**
   - *Role:* To realize a vision.
   - *Visuals:* Covered in paint/dust, surrounded by tools, intense concentration, eclectic fashion.
8. **The Ruler:**
   - *Role:* To create a prosperous, successful community.
   - *Visuals:* Impeccable tailoring, symmetrical framing, sitting at the head of a table, looking down slightly.
9. **The Caregiver:**
   - *Role:* To protect and care for others.
   - *Visuals:* Soft fabrics (knits), warm lighting, physical contact (hugging, holding hands), warm smiles.
10. **The Everyman (Regular Guy/Gal):**
    - *Role:* To connect with others and belong.
    - *Visuals:* Flannel, jeans, approachable posture, messy environments.
11. **The Jester:**
    - *Role:* To live in the moment with full enjoyment.
    - *Visuals:* Bright, clashing colors, highly exaggerated expressions, dynamic mid-air poses.
12. **The Lover:**
    - *Role:* To attain intimacy and experience sensual pleasure.
    - *Visuals:* Silk, velvet, shallow depth of field, extreme close-ups on lips/eyes, low-key warm lighting.

Usage in Advertising
- Identify the brand's archetype first (from `brand_rules/identity.md`), then cast the protagonist to match that archetype.
