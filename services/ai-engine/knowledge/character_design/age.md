# Character Age Guide

Goal
Define the age of the character accurately to ensure the visual representation matches the target demographic and story requirements.

Age Categories & Visual Markers
- **Child (0-12):** Focus on wide eyes, softer features, and slightly exaggerated proportions if stylized. Wardrobe is often bright and playful.
- **Teenager (13-19):** Transitional features. Often styled in contemporary trends. Use terms like "adolescent," "youthful," or "Gen Z aesthetics."
- **Young Adult (20-35):** The most common demographic in commercial advertising. Focus on sharp features, clear skin, and aspirational styling.
- **Middle-Aged (36-55):** Focus on lived-in features. Subtle laugh lines, salt-and-pepper hair. Prompts should include "mature," "distinguished," or "established."
- **Senior (56+):** Focus on texture, wisdom, and character. Prompts should specify "elderly," "silver hair," "deep wrinkles," and "gentle eyes."

Prompting Advice
- Avoid simply using a number (e.g., "a 35-year-old"). AI often misinterprets exact ages.
- Use descriptive combinations: "A young adult in their late 20s, youthful energy" or "A distinguished middle-aged man in his 50s, greying temples."

Consistency Note
When generating multiple shots of the same character, maintaining the exact age markers (wrinkles, hairline, skin texture) is critical to avoid the character looking like they are aging or de-aging between cuts.
