# Character Fashion & Wardrobe Guide

Goal
Use clothing to instantly communicate a character's personality, socio-economic status, profession, or state of mind before they even speak.

Wardrobe Categories
- **High Fashion / Luxury:** Tailored suits, silk dresses, minimalist avant-garde designs. Use tokens: "haute couture," "bespoke tailoring," "monochrome."
- **Streetwear / Urban:** Oversized hoodies, graphic tees, limited-edition sneakers. Use tokens: "hypebeast," "urban streetwear," "layered clothing."
- **Business Casual / Corporate:** Blazers, button-downs, slacks, clean silhouettes. Use tokens: "smart casual," "modern office wear."
- **Bohemian / Earthy:** Flowing fabrics, earthy tones, layered jewelry, linen. Use tokens: "boho chic," "organic fabrics," "vintage."
- **Athleisure / Sport:** Technical fabrics, form-fitting workout gear, neon accents. Use tokens: "performance wear," "athletic apparel."

Color Psychology in Fashion
- **Red:** Power, passion, or danger. (Draws the eye instantly).
- **Blue:** Trust, calm, corporate reliability.
- **Black:** Elegance, mystery, mourning, or edge.
- **White:** Purity, cleanliness, modernity.
- **Earth Tones (Browns/Greens):** Approachability, groundedness, eco-consciousness.

Prompting Advice
- Always describe the fit of the clothing (e.g., "baggy," "tailored," "flowing").
- Mention the material (e.g., "leather jacket," "silk blouse," "coarse wool sweater"). This helps the AI generate realistic textures.
- Keep the wardrobe description consistent across scenes unless a story beat dictates a costume change.
