# Character Expressions Guide

Goal
Convey the internal emotional state of the character clearly to the audience through facial micro-expressions.

Key Expressions & Prompt Tokens
- **Joy / Elation:** "Wide genuine smile, crinkled eyes (Duchenne smile), radiant expression."
- **Subtle Confidence:** "Slight smirk, relaxed brow, intense eye contact, calm demeanor."
- **Sorrow / Melancholy:** "Downcast eyes, slight frown, relaxed facial muscles, single tear, looking away."
- **Anger / Intensity:** "Furrowed brow, narrowed eyes, clenched jaw, flared nostrils."
- **Surprise / Awe:** "Slightly parted lips, wide eyes, raised eyebrows, illuminated face."
- **Contemplation:** "Looking off-camera, resting chin on hand, relaxed eyes, deep thought."

Directing the AI
- Avoid generic terms like "happy" or "sad." Be specific about *what* the face is doing.
- Example: Instead of "a happy man," use "A man looking at a product with a warm, approving smile and relaxed eyes."
- **The Kuleshov Effect:** Remember that an expression's meaning changes based on what the character is looking at (the previous or next shot). Sometimes a completely neutral, "deadpan" expression is the most powerful choice.

Avoid
- Over-exaggerated, cartoonish expressions in live-action or realistic styles.
- "Dead eyes" (always specify lighting hitting the eyes, e.g., "catchlights in eyes").
