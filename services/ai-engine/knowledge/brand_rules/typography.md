# Typography Guide

Goal
Standardize the visual representation of text within the video, ensuring readability and brand alignment.

Hierarchy
1. **Primary Typeface:** Used for large, impactful text (Main Titles, Hooks, CTAs). Must be highly legible and carry the core personality of the brand.
2. **Secondary Typeface:** Used for supporting text (Subtitles, disclaimers, specs). Usually a clean sans-serif designed for readability at small sizes.

On-Screen Text Rules
- **Safe Zones:** Keep all critical text within title-safe and action-safe margins to prevent cropping on different devices (especially critical for 9:16 social media).
- **Contrast & Legibility:** Always ensure high contrast between text and background. Use subtle drop shadows, edge glows, or semi-transparent background plates if the video behind the text is busy.
- **Duration:** Leave text on screen long enough for a viewer to read it aloud twice.

Application in Video
- **Kinetic Typography:** If the ad relies on animated text, the motion should match the brand voice (e.g., smooth and slow for luxury; snappy and bouncy for Gen Z ecommerce).
- **Lower Thirds:** Standardize the location, font size, and color for any name or title cards.

Avoid
- Mixing more than two font families.
- Overusing all-caps for long paragraphs.
- Placing text directly over a subject's face.
- Using default system fonts (Arial, Times New Roman) unless it's an intentional design choice.
