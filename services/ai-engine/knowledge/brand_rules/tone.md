# Brand Tone Guide

Goal
Define the emotional attitude of the brand's communication for a specific project or context. 

Voice vs. Tone
- **Voice** is the personality (constant).
- **Tone** is the mood or attitude (variable depending on the context).
  *Example: A brand's voice is 'Helpful and Expert'. Its tone in a product launch might be 'Excited and Visionary', while its tone in an apology video must be 'Serious and Empathetic'.*

Tone Spectrum Dimensions
Assess where the brand sits on these scales for a given project:
1. **Humorous vs. Serious:** Slapstick, dry wit, or deeply earnest?
2. **Formal vs. Casual:** Professional and structured, or conversational and relaxed?
3. **Respectful vs. Irreverent:** Safe and polite, or provocative and boundary-pushing?
4. **Enthusiastic vs. Matter-of-Fact:** High-energy sales pitch, or calm, confident statement?

Application in Production
- **Acting & Performance:** Instruct the talent (or AI character) on their demeanor. A humorous tone requires expressive facial animations and comedic timing.
- **Music Selection:** The most powerful driver of tone. A serious tone requires atmospheric or orchestral music; an irreverent tone might use quirky percussion or silence.
- **Editing Pacing:** Enthusiastic tones require fast, rhythmic cuts. Matter-of-fact tones benefit from longer, deliberate takes.

Checklist for Agents
- [ ] Does the tone match the specific goal of this advertisement/video?
- [ ] Is there a tonal disconnect between the music, the script, and the visual style?
