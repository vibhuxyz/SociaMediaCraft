# Brand Voice Guide

Goal
Ensure the spoken, written, and implied voice of the brand is consistently represented across all video assets.

Definition
Brand Voice is the personality and style with which a brand communicates. It remains consistent even when the tone changes.

Core Traits to Define
- **Archetype:** Is the brand a Caregiver, a Rebel, a Hero, or a Sage?
- **Vocabulary:** Does the brand use elevated, academic language or casual, street-level slang?
- **Pronouns:** Does the brand say "We" (corporate), "I" (founder-led), or address the viewer directly as "You" (empowering)?
- **Pacing:** Are sentences short and punchy, or long and poetic?

Application in Production
- **Screenplay Dialogue:** All dialogue must filter through the brand voice. A luxury brand character speaks with brevity; a social media brand character speaks with high-energy trending phrases.
- **Voiceover (VO):** The script for the narrator must embody this voice. E.g., an outdoors brand needs gritty, grounded sentences, not polished corporate jargon.
- **On-Screen Text (GFX):** Captions, supers, and title cards must use the vocabulary dictated by the brand voice.

Checklist for Agents
- [ ] Does the dialogue sound like it came from the same "person"?
- [ ] Are we avoiding buzzwords if the brand is meant to be authentic?
- [ ] Is the call-to-action (CTA) phrased in a way that matches the brand's established relationship with the audience?
