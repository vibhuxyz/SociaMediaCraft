# Cafe Environments Guide

Goal
Establish a relatable, cozy, or bustling "third place" (neither home nor work) that is universally understood in advertising.

Variations
- **The Indie Coffee Shop:** Exposed brick, edison bulbs, mismatched furniture, latte art, hipsters, warm dim lighting. (Good for lifestyle, creative tech).
- **The Parisian Bistro:** Wicker chairs, small round tables, outdoor seating facing the street, awnings, natural light. (Good for fashion, romance, travel).
- **The Modern Bakery/Matcha Bar:** Minimalist, pastel or stark white, terrazzo countertops, bright even lighting. (Good for skincare, wellness, Gen Z brands).

AI Prompting Tokens
- "cozy indie coffee shop interior, warm edison bulb lighting, exposed brick"
- "Parisian street cafe, outdoor seating, morning sunlight"
- "bright minimalist matcha bar, terrazzo countertops, soft natural light"

Set Dressing Details
- Always include props: steaming mugs, laptops, pastries, newspapers.
