# Luxury Locations Guide

Goal
Provide a backdrop of undeniable exclusivity and wealth that elevates the product.

Key Environments
- **The Modern Villa:** Cliffside, infinity pool, floor-to-ceiling glass, stark white geometry.
- **The Heritage Estate:** French chateau, English manor, manicured hedges, grand staircases, chandeliers.
- **The High-End Hotel/Lounge:** Velvet booths, brass accents, dim moody lighting, martinis, piano.
- **The Superyacht:** Teak decks, deep blue ocean, chrome fittings, brilliant sunshine.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "modern cliffside luxury villa, infinity pool, sunset"
- "interior of a grand European estate, crystal chandelier, marble floors"
- "exclusive VIP lounge, velvet seating, dim moody lighting, brass accents"

Avoid
- Clutter. True luxury in advertising is often defined by the *absence* of things (vast space, clean surfaces).
- Gaudy, overly shiny gold everywhere (unless the brand specifically requests a "nouveau riche" aesthetic).
