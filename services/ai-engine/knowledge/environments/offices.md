# Office Environments Guide

Goal
Communicate the type of work and the culture of a company visually.

Variations
- **The Startup / Creative Agency:** Open plan, glass walls, bean bag chairs, whiteboards, plants, casual dress, bright natural light.
- **The Corporate High-Rise:** Floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a city skyline, mahogany desks or sleek metal/glass, leather chairs, cooler/sterile lighting.
- **The Home Office:** Cozy, personalized, a mix of domestic and professional (bookshelves, a cat, a coffee mug next to a monitor).
- **The Warehouse / Maker Space:** High ceilings, industrial shelving, concrete floors, focused task lighting.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "modern open-plan creative office, lush indoor plants, bright sunlight"
- "executive corner office, city skyline view, sleek glass desk"
- "cozy home office desk setup, warm ambient lighting"

Avoid
- "Cubicle farms" (unless parodying corporate drudgery, as this evokes depression and boredom).
