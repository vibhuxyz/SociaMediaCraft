# Architectural Environments Guide

Goal
Use built structures to convey a specific era, mood, or level of sophistication.

Architectural Styles
- **Brutalism:** Heavy, raw concrete, imposing, geometric. Excellent for high-fashion or dystopian tech.
- **Mid-Century Modern:** Wood paneling, clean lines, large glass windows. Excellent for sophisticated lifestyle brands.
- **Gothic / Classical:** Ornate, historical, heavy stone. Excellent for heritage luxury (watches, whiskey).
- **Ultra-Modern / Parametric:** Curved glass, steel, fluid shapes. Excellent for futuristic tech or electric vehicles.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "brutalist architecture, raw concrete walls, imposing geometry"
- "mid-century modern home, floor-to-ceiling windows, teak wood"
- "classical European architecture, ornate molding, marble columns"

Avoid
- Generic "buildings." If you don't specify the architectural style, AI will generate bizarre, structurally impossible mashups.
