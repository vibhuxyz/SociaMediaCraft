# Indoor Environments Guide

Goal
Design interior spaces that reflect the character's lifestyle, socioeconomic status, or the brand's aesthetic without feeling like a generic "studio set."

Key Considerations
- **Set Dressing (Clutter vs. Clean):** A lived-in space requires deliberate clutter (magazines, coffee cups, coats draped over chairs). A high-end minimal space requires pure geometry and zero clutter.
- **Motivated Lighting:** Indoors, light must come from a source (a window, a lamp, a TV screen). Always define this source.
- **Texture:** Wallpaper, exposed brick, smooth concrete, or soft fabrics.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "detailed interior design, soft ambient room lighting, realistic set dressing"
- "cozy living room, soft textiles, warm lamp light"
- "minimalist interior, stark walls, geometric furniture"

Avoid
- Empty, featureless rooms (the AI will generate "liminal spaces" that feel unsettling).
- Conflicting light sources (e.g., bright sunlight coming from a wall with no window).
