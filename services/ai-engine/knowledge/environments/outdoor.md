# Outdoor Environments Guide

Goal
Utilize exterior locations to establish scale, season, and atmosphere.

Key Considerations
- **Time of Day:** Outdoors, the sun is your primary light source. You must always define the time of day (Golden Hour, Blue Hour, High Noon, Midnight).
- **Weather/Atmosphere:** Clear skies are boring. Add atmosphere to create depth (fog, rain, mist, overcast skies, or smog).
- **Scale:** Use wide shots to show the subject's relationship to the vast outdoors.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "exterior shot, overcast day, atmospheric mist"
- "golden hour sunlight filtering through trees"
- "urban exterior, neon reflections in puddles, nighttime"

Avoid
- "Perfect" weather unless it's a specific brand requirement (like a summer vacation ad).
- Flat lighting at high noon (unless going for a harsh, gritty look).
