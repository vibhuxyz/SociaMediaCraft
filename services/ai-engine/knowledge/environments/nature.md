# Nature Environments Guide

Goal
Evoke freedom, purity, adventure, or raw power through untouched landscapes.

Key Environments
- **The Deep Forest:** Pine trees, fog, shafts of light, moss. (Mysterious, earthy, masculine).
- **The Arid Desert:** Sand dunes, cracked earth, harsh sun, heat distortion. (Endurance, heat, vastness).
- **The Alpine Mountain:** Snow-capped peaks, crisp blue skies, sheer drops. (Achievement, extreme sports, fresh air).
- **The Tropical Coast:** Turquoise water, white sand, palm fronds, golden sun. (Relaxation, escape, joy).

AI Prompting Tokens
- "dense misty pine forest, shafts of sunlight piercing through branches"
- "vast sweeping sand dunes, harsh midday sun, cinematic desert"
- "snow-capped mountain peaks, crisp clear sky, high altitude"

Avoid
- "A park." Man-made nature (parks, lawns) feels domestic. If you want adventure, you must prompt for true wilderness.
