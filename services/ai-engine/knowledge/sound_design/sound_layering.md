# Sound Layering Guide

Goal
Create a rich, three-dimensional soundscape by combining multiple audio elements rather than relying on a single sound effect.

Theory
A single sound effect usually sounds thin and fake. Real world sounds are complex aggregates of multiple frequencies.

The "Car Drive By" Example
If you just prompt "car driving by," it will sound generic. You must layer:
1. **Low Frequency:** The engine rumble (Sub-bass).
2. **Mid Frequency:** The tire friction on the asphalt.
3. **High Frequency:** The wind rushing past the chassis (Whoosh).

The "Product Reveal" Example
1. **The Bed:** A slow, rising musical drone.
2. **The Action:** The crisp foley of the product unlocking or opening.
3. **The Sweetener:** A subtle, magical "shimmer" or "chime" sound to subconsciously signal value and luxury.

Instruction for Sound Designer Agent
When planning the audio for a shot, never list just one sound. List the *Foreground* (Foley), the *Midground* (Action/Effects), and the *Background* (Ambience).
