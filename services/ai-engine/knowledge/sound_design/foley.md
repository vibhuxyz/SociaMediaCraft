# Foley Guide

Goal
Ground AI-generated video in physical reality by adding highly specific, hyper-realistic synchronized sound effects.

Theory
Because AI video generation is inherently silent (and slightly uncanny), Foley is the secret weapon to make it feel real. When the audience *hears* the exact texture of a leather jacket moving, their brain accepts the visual as real.

Essential Foley Categories in Advertising
1. **Footsteps:** Must match the shoe type (heels, sneakers, boots) and the surface (concrete, gravel, hardwood).
2. **Cloth Movements (Rustle):** Silk sounds different than heavy denim. This adds subtle life to static shots.
3. **Product Interaction:** The crisp *crack* of a soda can, the heavy *thud* of a luxury car door closing, the sharp *click* of a lipstick tube.
