# Audio Mixing Rules Guide

Goal
Ensure the final commercial is perfectly legible on all devices (especially mobile phones) and meets broadcast standards.

The Audio Hierarchy
When the final mix is rendered, elements must be prioritized in this order:
1. **The Voice (VO / Dialogue):** MUST be the loudest and clearest element. Nothing can compete with the human voice.
2. **Foley & Sound Effects:** Punctuate the action, sitting just below the voice.
3. **Music:** Provides the emotional bed. It should "duck" (lower in volume) whenever the Voiceover is speaking.
4. **Ambience:** Sits at the absolute bottom.

Volume Targets (General Guidelines)
- **Overall Master (LUFS):** Target -14 LUFS for YouTube/Social Media. Target -23 LUFS for Broadcast TV.
- **Voiceover:** Peaks around -3dB to -6dB.
- **Music (Under Voice):** -18dB to -24dB (so it doesn't mask the voice).
- **Music (During breaks in Voice):** -10dB to -12dB.
- **Sound Effects:** Varies, but Impacts can peak around -6dB.

The "Phone Test"
The Sound Designer must always remember: 70% of the audience will hear this on a terrible smartphone speaker. Do not rely entirely on sub-bass (like a deep boom) because a phone speaker literally cannot produce those frequencies. You must add mid-range "crunch" or texture so it can be heard on an iPhone.
