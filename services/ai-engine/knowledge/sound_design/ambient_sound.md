# Ambient Sound (Room Tone) Guide

Goal
Establish the spatial environment of the scene before any action or music takes place.

Theory
No room is truly silent. A completely silent audio track underneath a voiceover sounds dead and artificial. Ambient sound (or "Room Tone") provides the subconscious baseline of the environment.

Types of Ambience
1. **The Office:** Subtle air conditioning hum, distant keyboards, faint phone ringing.
2. **The Cafe:** Indistinct chatter, espresso machine steaming, cups clinking in the background.
3. **The Outdoors (Nature):** Wind through trees, specific birds (crows for spooky, seagulls for beach, songbirds for morning), distant water.
4. **The Outdoors (Urban):** Distant traffic roar, faint sirens, footsteps, subway rumble.

Mixing Rule
Ambience should sit at the very bottom of the mix (around -30dB to -40dB). It should be felt rather than actively heard, unless it is specifically called out in the script.

AI Audio Prompting Tokens
- "ambient room tone, bustling Parisian cafe, indistinct chatter, distant espresso machine"
- "quiet forest ambience, gentle wind rustling leaves, distant songbirds"
