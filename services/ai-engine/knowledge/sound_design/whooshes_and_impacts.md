# Whooshes, Impacts, and Risers Guide

Goal
Add cinematic energy to visual transitions, graphics, and logos.

Theory
In modern advertising (especially fast-paced social media ads), visual cuts and text animations need audio support to feel impactful.

Key Sound Design Elements

1. **The Whoosh (Swoosh/Swish):**
   - *Use:* To bridge two fast cuts (whip pans) or to introduce text flying onto the screen.
   - *Variations:* "Subtle air whoosh" (for clean tech), "Aggressive low-end whoosh" (for sports).

2. **The Riser (Build-up):**
   - *Use:* To build extreme tension over 3-5 seconds right before a climax or beat drop.
   - *Variations:* Synthesizer sweep, reverse cymbal, orchestral strings rising in pitch.

3. **The Impact (Boom/Hit):**
   - *Use:* For the final logo reveal, a massive realization, or a hard cut to black.
   - *Variations:* "Deep cinematic sub-boom" (Inception style), "Sharp metallic hit" (Sci-fi).

4. **The Glitch / Stutter:**
   - *Use:* For tech, gaming, or Gen-Z fashion brands to show rapid, chaotic cuts.
