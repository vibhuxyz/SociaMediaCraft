# Corporate Clean Visual Style Guide

Goal
Build ultimate trust, reliability, and approachability for B2B, SaaS, financial, and healthcare brands.

Core Aesthetic
Bright, flawless, diverse, and universally inoffensive.

Visual Pillars
- **Environment:** Modern glass-and-steel offices, bright white studios, or tidy home workspaces.
- **Lighting:** Soft, flattering, high-key three-point lighting. No moody shadows.
- **Color Palette:** Dominated by the brand's primary color (usually blue for trust, or green for growth) offset by clean whites and grays.
- **Casting:** Friendly, approachable, smiling, casually professional (blazers and t-shirts, not stiff suits).
