# High Fashion / Editorial Visual Style Guide

Goal
Create an avant-garde, intimidatingly stylish, and hyper-premium aesthetic that prioritizes artistic expression over product clarity.

Core Aesthetic
Confidence, wealth, abstraction, and striking poses.

Visual Pillars
- **Lighting:** Can be extreme. Either totally flat (hard flash against a white wall) or intensely sculpted (single spotlight in pitch black).
- **Casting & Posing:** Subjects do not smile. Poses are angular, unnatural, and confident.
- **Environment:** Often surreal or highly abstracted (e.g., a model standing in a completely empty concrete bunker, or a stark desert).
- **Wardrobe:** Avant-garde, structural, bold.
