# Cyberpunk Visual Style Guide

Goal
Create a high-tech, dystopian, or heavily digitized environment that appeals to gamers, tech enthusiasts, and streetwear brands.

Core Aesthetic
"High Tech, Low Life." The juxtaposition of advanced technology against gritty, decaying urban environments.

Visual Pillars
- **Lighting:** Neon practicals (pink, cyan, purple, lime green) reflecting off wet surfaces (rain, puddles).
- **Architecture:** Towering brutalist skyscrapers, cramped alleys, holographic billboards, exposed wiring.
- **Atmosphere:** Heavy smog, steam, continuous rain, and darkness.
- **Color Grading:** Deep crushed blacks with hyper-saturated neon highlights.
