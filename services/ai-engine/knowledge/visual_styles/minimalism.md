# Minimalism Visual Style Guide

Goal
Communicate extreme focus, premium quality, and effortless elegance by stripping away absolutely everything that is not essential.

Core Aesthetic
"Less, but better." Rooted in Scandinavian design and Apple's brand identity.

Visual Pillars
- **Environment:** Stark, empty, geometric. Vast negative space.
- **Lighting:** Even, soft, high-key, with no harsh or distracting shadows.
- **Color Palette:** Monochromatic, heavily relying on white, beige, light gray, or a single muted pastel.
- **Subjects:** Singular focus. One object. One person. No clutter.
