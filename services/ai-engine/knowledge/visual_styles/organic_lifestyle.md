# Organic Lifestyle Visual Style Guide

Goal
Communicate sustainability, health, authenticity, and connection to nature.

Core Aesthetic
Earthy, imperfect, warm, and sun-drenched.

Visual Pillars
- **Environment:** Nature, cozy homes, farmers markets, wooden textures, linen.
- **Lighting:** Pure natural sunlight, preferably Golden Hour or dappled light through trees.
- **Color Palette:** Earth tones (terracotta, olive green, warm beige, mustard).
- **Texture:** Matte finishes, natural fibers, zero plastic, zero neon.
