# Retro / Vintage Visual Style Guide

Goal
Trigger instant nostalgia by mimicking the imperfections of analog technology from a specific decade (70s, 80s, 90s).

Core Aesthetic
Warm, lived-in, imperfect, and deeply human.

Visual Pillars
- **Texture:** Heavy 35mm or 16mm film grain, dust, scratches, VHS tracking lines.
- **Color Grading:** Faded blacks (milky shadows), halation (red glow around highlights), shift toward yellow/green.
- **Wardrobe/Props:** Period-accurate styling is mandatory (e.g., tube TVs, rotary phones, corduroy).
- **Lenses:** Vintage anamorphic or soft spherical lenses. No ultra-sharp digital edges.
