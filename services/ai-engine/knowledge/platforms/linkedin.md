# LinkedIn Platform Guide

Goal
Deliver professional, value-driven, B2B content that builds authority, trust, and career-oriented engagement.

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratios:** 1:1 (Square), 4:5 (Portrait), or 16:9 (Horizontal).
- **Audio:** "Sound Off" optimization is highly recommended.
- **Duration:** 30s - 60s+.

Creative Strategy
- **Tone:** Professional, educational, inspiring, or corporate. Avoid overly aggressive direct-response sales tactics.
- **The Hook:** Start with a bold industry statistic, a thought-provoking question, or a career-related pain point.
- **Visuals:** High-end office environments, professional attire, clean graphics, and data visualizations.
- **Trust Elements:** Feature real people (or highly realistic AI representations) looking directly into the camera.

AI Prompting Notes
- "Corporate environment, professional lighting, clean modern office."
- Avoid frantic camera movements. Use static shots, smooth sliders, and medium close-ups to convey authority.
