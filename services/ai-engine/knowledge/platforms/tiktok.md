# TikTok Platform Guide

Goal
Create highly engaging, trend-driven, and authentic video content that prioritizes entertainment over aesthetic perfection. "Don't make ads. Make TikToks."

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratio:** strictly 9:16 (Vertical).
- **Audio:** "Sound On" is mandatory. Music and trending audio are the backbone of the platform.
- **Duration:** 9s - 15s is ideal for ads.

Creative Strategy
- **The Hook:** You have less than 2 seconds. The first frame must be visually confusing, extremely loud, or highly relatable.
- **Lo-Fi Aesthetic:** High-budget cinematic lighting often fails on TikTok because it triggers "Ad Blindness." Use UGC (User Generated Content) styles, handheld camera movements, and selfie angles.
- **Editing:** Fast, jump-cut pacing. Cut out all dead air and breathing.
- **Text:** Bold, native-looking text overlays.

AI Prompting Notes
- Prompt for "iPhone footage, front-facing camera, handheld vlog style."
- Center the subject tightly. The bottom 20% and right edge of the screen are completely covered by TikTok UI elements. Do not place anything important there.
