# Facebook Platform Guide

Goal
Capture the attention of a slightly older, community-driven demographic using clear value propositions and sound-off optimization.

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratios:** 1:1 (Square) or 4:5 (Portrait) for Feed.
- **Audio:** 85% of Facebook video is watched with SOUND OFF. Visual storytelling and captions are absolutely mandatory.
- **Duration:** 15s - 30s.

Creative Strategy
- **Direct Messaging:** Don't rely on subtle metaphors. State the problem and the solution clearly in the first 3 seconds.
- **Text-Heavy:** Use bold banners, subtitles, and text callouts to explain the product without sound.
- **Demographic Appeal:** Leans toward families, older millennials, and Gen X. Imagery should be relatable, grounded, and trustworthy.

AI Prompting Notes
- Use 1:1 or 4:5 framing.
- Leave ample negative space (copy space) for large text overlays.
- Bright, high-key lighting works best to stand out against the white/dark mode feed background.
