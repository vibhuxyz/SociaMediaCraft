# Instagram Platform Guide

Goal
Create visually arresting, aesthetic-first content that blends seamlessly with high-quality influencer and peer content.

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratios:** 9:16 (Reels/Stories), 4:5 (Feed portrait), 1:1 (Square).
- **Audio:** "Sound On" is critical for Reels and Stories; "Sound Off" readability is required for Feed.
- **Duration:** 15s - 60s is the sweet spot.

Creative Strategy
- **Aesthetic First:** Instagram users value beauty, lifestyle, and curation. The lighting, wardrobe, and color grading must feel premium.
- **The Hook:** You have 2-3 seconds to stop the scroll. Use rapid movement, striking colors, or an immediate face-to-camera intro.
- **Captions:** On-screen text is mandatory, but it must be styled elegantly (not generic closed-captions).
- **Native Look:** Content shot on "iPhone Cinematic" often outperforms highly produced RED/ARRI footage because it feels like native platform content.

AI Prompting Notes
- Frame for 9:16 or 4:5. Center-punch the subjects.
- Ensure the right side of the screen is clear for Reels UI (Likes/Comments).
