# X (Twitter) Platform Guide

Goal
Capitalize on real-time trends, news cycles, and fast-paced scrolling with punchy, controversial, or highly timely video content.

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratios:** 16:9 (Horizontal) or 1:1 (Square).
- **Audio:** Often viewed without sound on mobile, but sound is heavily used for memes/entertainment.
- **Duration:** 6s - 15s (Short loops perform best).

Creative Strategy
- **The Hook:** Needs to immediately spark outrage, laughter, or intense curiosity. X is a text-first platform; the video must visually justify interrupting the user's reading flow.
- **Meme Culture:** Visuals that can be screenshotted and turned into memes perform exceptionally well.
- **Pacing:** Extremely fast. Get straight to the point.
- **Visuals:** High contrast, arresting imagery.

AI Prompting Notes
- Frame for 16:9 or 1:1.
- Focus on striking, unusual, or highly shareable imagery (the "thumb-stopper").
