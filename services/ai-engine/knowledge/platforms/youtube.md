# YouTube Platform Guide

Goal
Optimize advertising content for long-form viewing behavior, unskippable constraints, and high-fidelity screens.

Format & Technical Constraints
- **Aspect Ratio:** Primarily 16:9 (Horizontal). Shorts are 9:16 (Vertical).
- **Resolution:** Up to 4K.
- **Audio:** High fidelity (users often watch on TVs or with good headphones).
- **Duration:** 15s (Non-skippable), 30s-3min+ (Skippable).

Creative Strategy (TrueView In-Stream)
- **The 5-Second Rule:** Viewers can skip after 5 seconds. The brand, value proposition, and the hook must be delivered *before* the skip button appears.
- **Pacing:** Once past the 5-second mark, pacing can slow down to tell a more traditional, cinematic story.
- **Visuals:** High production value is rewarded. Treat this like television advertising.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** Must verbally and visually direct the user to click the link on the screen (Companion Banner).

AI Prompting Notes
- Frame for 16:9. Rule of Thirds is highly effective.
- Keep the bottom 10% of the screen clear of crucial text (play bar overlay).
