# Teal and Orange Color Grading

Goal
Achieve the classic Hollywood blockbuster look that maximizes color contrast and makes human subjects pop out from the background.

Theory
Teal and orange sit opposite each other on the color wheel (complementary colors). Since human skin tones naturally fall in the orange/red spectrum, pushing the shadows and backgrounds into the teal/blue spectrum creates maximum visual separation and depth.

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Pushed toward cyan/teal.
- **Midtones/Skin:** Pushed toward warm orange or gold.
- **Highlights:** Kept relatively clean or slightly warm.
- **Contrast:** Typically high contrast to emphasize the color separation.

Ideal Use Cases
- Action and Sci-Fi films/ads.
- Automotive commercials.
- Any project needing an instantly recognizable "cinematic" and high-budget feel.
