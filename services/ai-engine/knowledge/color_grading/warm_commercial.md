# Warm Commercial Color Grading

Goal
Create an inviting, cozy, and aspirational look that makes the viewer feel comfortable and happy.

Theory
Warm colors (yellows, oranges, soft reds) evoke feelings of sunlight, morning, and domestic comfort. This grade often reduces harsh contrast to make the image feel softer and more approachable.

Visual Characteristics
- **Overall Cast:** A slight golden or amber tint across the entire image.
- **Shadows:** Lifted (milky) and infused with warmth rather than pure black.
- **Highlights:** Soft, blooming, often mimicking early morning sunlight.
- **Saturation:** Moderately high, especially for food, skin, and wood textures.

Ideal Use Cases
- Coffee and Breakfast Ads
- Family-oriented commercials
- Real Estate (showing cozy living spaces)
- Pet products
