# Luxury Gold Color Grading

Goal
Communicate wealth, exclusivity, and premium quality through a rich, sophisticated color palette.

Theory
Gold and deep black are universally associated with premium materials (gold jewelry, black leather, tuxedoes). By restricting the color palette to these tones, the image feels heavily curated and expensive.

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Deep, crushed blacks. Very clean, no color tint in the darkest areas.
- **Highlights/Midtones:** Shimmering gold, brass, or champagne hues.
- **Saturation:** Desaturated overall, EXCEPT for the gold/yellow channel which is highly emphasized.
- **Contrast:** Very high. Striking differences between the deep shadows and the bright metallic highlights.

Ideal Use Cases
- Perfume and Cologne Ads
- High-end watches and jewelry
- Luxury automotive
- Premium liquor brands
