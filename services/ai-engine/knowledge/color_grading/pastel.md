# Pastel Color Grading

Goal
Create a soft, dreamy, whimsical, or highly stylized "pop" aesthetic by removing harsh contrasts and relying on light, desaturated colors.

Theory
Pastels are colors with high luminance (lightness) and low to medium saturation. This creates an airy, innocent, or retro-futuristic look (often associated with Wes Anderson or K-Pop aesthetics).

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Heavily lifted. True black rarely exists in this grade; shadows are usually tinted soft purple, blue, or pink.
- **Highlights:** Soft and expansive.
- **Colors:** Mint green, baby blue, soft pink, lavender, and butter yellow.
- **Contrast:** Low. The image feels "flat" but intentionally so.

Ideal Use Cases
- Spring/Summer fashion campaigns
- Cosmetics and skincare
- Quirky, comedic, or surreal advertising
- Gen-Z focused lifestyle products
