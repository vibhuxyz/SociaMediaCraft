# Vintage / Retro Color Grading

Goal
Evoke nostalgia and mimic the imperfections of analog film stock from specific eras (70s, 80s, 90s).

Theory
Analog film naturally degrades, shifts in color, and contains physical imperfections. Replicating this digitally creates immediate emotional resonance and a "lived-in" feel.

Visual Characteristics
- **Texture:** Heavy film grain, potential scratches, or dust.
- **Shadows:** Faded (milky) and often tinted green or magenta.
- **Highlights:** Creamy or off-white, sometimes exhibiting halation (a red/orange glow around bright edges).
- **Saturation:** Either muted (like faded photographs) or overly saturated (mimicking cheap 80s videotape or Kodachrome).
- **Vignetting:** Darker edges drawing the eye to the center.

Ideal Use Cases
- Heritage brands (clothing, boots, whiskey)
- Indie music videos
- Nostalgia-driven marketing (e.g., retro cameras, polaroids)
