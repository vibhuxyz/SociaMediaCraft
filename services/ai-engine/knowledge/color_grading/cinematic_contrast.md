# Cinematic Contrast Color Grading

Goal
Create drama, shape, and three-dimensionality by relying on heavily contrasting luminance rather than extreme color shifts.

Theory
Contrast defines shape. This grade is about finding the darkest point and the brightest point in the frame and ensuring a steep, dramatic roll-off between them, creating a "sculpted" look.

Visual Characteristics
- **Shadows:** Dark and rich, often completely black in the deepest areas.
- **Highlights:** Bright but carefully protected (not blown out to pure white).
- **Midtones:** Often slightly compressed or lowered to maintain a moody atmosphere.
- **Color:** Colors remain relatively natural and grounded, letting the luminance contrast do the heavy lifting.

Ideal Use Cases
- Dramatic storytelling
- Fitness and athletic commercials (highlights muscle definition)
- Moody architectural or fashion shoots
