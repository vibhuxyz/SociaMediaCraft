# Advertising Storytelling Guide

Goal
Compress a compelling narrative arc into 15 to 60 seconds while ensuring the brand is the ultimate hero or facilitator of the resolution.

Theory
Nobody wants to watch an ad; they want to watch a story. The product should not be the *subject* of the story; it should be the *tool* that allows the human subject to overcome their obstacle.

The Advertising Arc
1. **The Hook (Seconds 0-3):** Establish the stakes, the mood, or a relatable pain point immediately.
2. **The Escalation (Seconds 3-15):** The problem gets worse, or the desire gets stronger.
3. **The Pivot (Seconds 15-20):** The product is introduced as the solution.
4. **The Payoff (Seconds 20-25):** We see the human character experiencing the positive emotional result of using the product.
5. **The Call to Action (Seconds 25-30):** Brand logo, tagline, and what the viewer should do next.

Rule for the Story Architect
Do not start an ad with "Hi, we are [Brand]." Start with the *customer's* world.
