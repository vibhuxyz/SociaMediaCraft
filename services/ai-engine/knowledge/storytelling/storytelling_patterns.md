# Storytelling Patterns (Frameworks)

Goal
Provide repeatable, proven narrative frameworks for rapid script generation.

The Frameworks

1. **PAS (Problem - Agitation - Solution):**
   - *Problem:* Show the pain point. (Your back hurts).
   - *Agitation:* Make it worse. (You can't pick up your kids).
   - *Solution:* The Brand. (Our ergonomic chair fixes your posture).

2. **Before / After / Bridge:**
   - *Before:* The sad, gray, difficult current reality.
   - *After:* The bright, happy, perfect future.
   - *Bridge:* The Brand is the bridge between the two.

3. **The "Day in the Life" (Vlog Style):**
   - Narrative told from a first-person perspective, integrating the product naturally into a chronological day.

4. **The Metaphor / Exaggeration:**
   - Taking a small problem and blowing it up to epic cinematic proportions (e.g., fighting a literal monster that represents "Monday Mornings").

Instructions for the Story Architect
When responding to a generic prompt, select one of these frameworks to instantly structure the narrative into a professional format.
