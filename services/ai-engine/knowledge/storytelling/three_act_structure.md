# Three-Act Structure (Micro-Narratives)

Goal
Apply classical dramatic structure to micro-narratives (short commercials).

Act 1: Setup (The Status Quo & Inciting Incident)
- **Duration:** 10-20% of the runtime.
- **Purpose:** Introduce the protagonist and their current (flawed or lacking) world. Establish empathy.
- *Ad Translation:* "You're tired. Your coffee isn't working."

Act 2: Confrontation (The Struggle)
- **Duration:** 50-60% of the runtime.
- **Purpose:** The protagonist tries to solve the problem and fails, or the tension builds.
- *Ad Translation:* "The commute is brutal. The rain is pouring. You are falling asleep at your desk."

Act 3: Resolution (The Climax & Aftermath)
- **Duration:** 20-30% of the runtime.
- **Purpose:** The protagonist finds the ultimate solution (The Product), overcomes the struggle, and enters a new, better Status Quo.
- *Ad Translation:* "You take a sip of [Brand Coffee]. Your eyes open wide. The sun comes out. You crush the presentation."
