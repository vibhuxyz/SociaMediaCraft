# The Hero's Journey (Brand Application)

Goal
Use Joseph Campbell's monomyth to position the customer as the Hero and the Brand as the Mentor.

The Core Mistake
Amateur brands position *themselves* as the Hero (e.g., "We are the best software company in the world!"). 
Professional brands position the *customer* as the Hero, and the brand as the magical Mentor (e.g., "You want to change the world. Here is the software to help you do it.")

The Advertising Monomyth Steps
1. **The Ordinary World:** The customer in their normal, unfulfilled life.
2. **The Call to Adventure:** A problem arises (The pain point).
3. **Meeting the Mentor:** The Brand appears, offering a magical tool (The Product).
4. **Crossing the Threshold:** The customer buys/uses the product.
5. **The Ordeal:** The customer successfully defeats their problem using the tool.
6. **The Return:** The customer returns to their normal life, transformed and victorious.

Prompting the Screenwriter
"Write a 30-second script where the customer is the Hero facing [Problem], and the Brand acts as the Mentor who provides the magical tool to defeat it."
