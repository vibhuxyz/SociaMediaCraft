# Pacing & Rhythm Guide

Goal
Control the flow of information to keep the viewer engaged without overwhelming them.

The Rubber Band Theory
Good pacing is like stretching and releasing a rubber band.
- **Tension (Fast):** Rapid cuts, building music, lots of information, conflict.
- **Release (Slow):** A long take, silence, a moment of realization, a deep breath.

Commercial Pacing Strategies
- **The Ramp-Up:** Starts slow (Act 1), gets faster and more chaotic (Act 2), and ends on a massive, fast climax before a sudden stop (The Logo).
- **The Cold Drop:** Starts at 100mph (Action/Chaos in the first 3 seconds), then suddenly slows down to explain *how* we got here (The Product explanation).
- **The Hypnotic Flow:** (Luxury/Perfume) Consistent, slow, deliberate pacing throughout. No rapid cuts. Every frame breathes.

Instructions for the Shot Planner
Match the shot lengths to the intended pacing. A "Ramp-Up" script should have 3-second shots at the beginning and 0.5-second shots at the climax.
