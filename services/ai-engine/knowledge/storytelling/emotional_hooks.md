# Emotional Hooks Guide

Goal
Bypass the viewer's logical brain and trigger an immediate emotional response in the first 3 seconds.

Types of Hooks

1. **The Curiosity Hook:**
   - *How:* Start with a visually confusing image or a paradox.
   - *Example:* A man is pouring coffee into a fishbowl. (The viewer *must* know why).

2. **The Empathy/Pain Hook:**
   - *How:* Show a universally relatable frustration.
   - *Example:* A phone battery dying right as the GPS gives the final instruction.

3. **The Aspirational Hook:**
   - *How:* Show the ultimate, perfect end-state of the target demographic.
   - *Example:* A perfectly tailored suit, a gorgeous sunset, incredible wealth.

4. **The Comedic/Shock Hook:**
   - *How:* Subvert expectations immediately with something absurd or mildly shocking.
   - *Example:* A grandmother doing a backflip.

Rule for the Story Architect
If the first 3 seconds do not contain one of these specific hooks, the script must be rewritten. Do not waste the first 3 seconds on a beautiful, slow landscape shot unless it is a high-end luxury ad.
