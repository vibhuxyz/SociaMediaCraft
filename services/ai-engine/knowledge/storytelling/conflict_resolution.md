# Conflict & Resolution Guide

Goal
Ensure every script has a distinct problem to solve. Without conflict, there is no story.

Types of Conflict in Advertising

1. **Character vs. Environment:**
   - *The Problem:* Weather, distance, time, physical obstacles.
   - *The Solution:* A rugged SUV, a waterproof jacket, a fast delivery app.

2. **Character vs. Society:**
   - *The Problem:* Judgment, fitting in, standing out, archaic rules.
   - *The Solution:* Edgy fashion, a disruptive tech platform, a bold makeup brand.

3. **Character vs. Self:**
   - *The Problem:* Doubt, fear, lack of energy, procrastination.
   - *The Solution:* Athletic wear (Just Do It), energy drinks, educational platforms.

4. **Character vs. Technology:**
   - *The Problem:* Broken Wi-Fi, complex software, lost data.
   - *The Solution:* A seamless SaaS product, a reliable telecom provider.

Rule for the Screenwriter
Clearly identify which of the 4 conflicts applies to the brand, and make that conflict the central antagonist of the script.
