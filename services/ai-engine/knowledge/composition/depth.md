# Depth (Foreground, Midground, Background) Guide

Goal
Create a three-dimensional illusion on a two-dimensional screen, making the image feel immersive and expensive.

Theory
A flat image looks cheap. A cinematic image has distinct layers:
1. **Foreground (FG):** Objects closest to the lens (often out of focus).
2. **Midground (MG):** Where the primary subject usually sits.
3. **Background (BG):** The environment behind the subject.

Application in Filmmaking
- **The "Dirty" Frame:** Shooting *past* an object in the foreground (like a lamp, a shoulder, or leaves) to give the viewer a sense of voyeurism and spatial awareness.
- **Depth of Field:** Using a shallow depth of field (blurry FG and BG) to separate the subject from the rest of the world.
- **Lighting for Depth:** Ensuring the background is lit differently (brighter or darker) than the subject so they don't blend together.
