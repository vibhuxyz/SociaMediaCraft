# Leading Lines Guide

Goal
Subconsciously direct the viewer's attention exactly where you want it to go by using the geometry of the environment.

Theory
Human eyes naturally follow lines. By utilizing roads, fences, architectural beams, shadows, or even the direction people are pointing, you can draw a literal line to the most important part of the frame (usually the product or the protagonist).

Application in Filmmaking
- **Converging Lines:** Pointing toward the center (e.g., looking down a long hallway or train track). This creates extreme depth and a sense of inevitability.
- **Diagonal Lines:** Create a sense of action, kinetic energy, and instability.
- **Curved (S-Curves):** Create a sense of elegance, journey, and smooth flow (very common in luxury car commercials winding through mountains).
