# Negative Space Guide

Goal
Use emptiness to isolate a subject, evoke emotion (loneliness, freedom), or provide functional space for graphic overlays.

Theory
Negative space is the area around and between the subjects of an image. "Vast" negative space shrinks the subject, making them look isolated, peaceful, or vulnerable, depending on the lighting.

Application in Advertising
- **The "Copy Space":** In commercials, negative space is a functional requirement. You need empty sky or a blank wall to place the brand's logo, the URL, or the CTA text without it becoming illegible.
- **Isolation:** A tiny car in a massive desert emphasizes the scale of the environment and the freedom of the journey.

AI Prompting Tokens
- "vast negative space, minimalist composition"
- "clean empty background, small subject in a large landscape"
- "copy space on the right side of the frame"

Avoid
- "Cluttered backgrounds." If the AI fills the negative space with irrelevant details, it ruins the intended isolation or renders the text overlay unreadable.
