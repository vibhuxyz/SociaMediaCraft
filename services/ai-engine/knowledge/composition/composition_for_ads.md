# Composition for Ads (Platform Optimization) Guide

Goal
Ensure the composition survives the cropping, UI overlays, and aspect ratios of modern digital platforms.

Theory
A beautiful cinematic 16:9 (widescreen) shot might be completely ruined when cropped to 9:16 (vertical) for TikTok, or when Instagram places a row of buttons over the right side of the screen.

Platform Constraints

1. **Social Media (9:16 Vertical):**
   - **The Action Safe Zone:** The middle 60% of the screen. 
   - **The Danger Zones:** The bottom 20% is covered by captions and account info. The right edge is covered by Like/Share buttons.
   - **Rule:** Keep the subject dead center. Do not use extreme "Rule of Thirds" on the edges, or the subject will be covered by UI.

2. **YouTube (16:9 Horizontal):**
   - **The Danger Zones:** The bottom right corner is covered by the video timestamp. The bottom 10% is covered by the play bar.
   - **Rule:** Traditional cinematic rules apply, but avoid placing logos in the extreme bottom right.

3. **Multi-Platform Delivery:**
   - If an ad is being shot for *both* 16:9 and 9:16, you must shoot "Center Punch." The subject is framed strictly in the center so the sides can be chopped off without losing the narrative.

AI Prompting Tokens for Vertical
- "vertical video framing, 9:16 aspect ratio"
- "subject tightly centered, optimized for mobile screen"
- "portrait orientation, no edge distractions"
