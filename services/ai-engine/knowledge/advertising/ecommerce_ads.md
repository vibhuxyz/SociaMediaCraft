# E-commerce Advertisement Guide

Goal
Drive immediate clicks and conversions by highlighting a problem and a rapid solution.

Target Emotion
- Urgency
- Curiosity
- Relief (problem solved)
- FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)

Typical Story Structure
1. The Hook (0-3s): Visually call out the audience or their problem immediately.
2. The Problem: Exaggerated frustration with the current state.
3. The Solution: Introduce the product as the hero.
4. The Demo: Clear, fast-paced demonstration of how it works.
5. Social Proof/Testimonial: Quick flash of stars or a happy customer.
6. Strong CTA: "Shop Now," "Link in Bio," "50% Off."

Visual Style
- Bright, well-lit, approachable.
- User-Generated Content (UGC) style or clean studio shots.
- On-screen text, captions, and graphics are mandatory.

Camera
- Often vertical (9:16) framing.
- Eye-level, feeling like a FaceTime or vlog.
- Fast cuts.

Music
- Upbeat, trendy, royalty-free stock music.
- Sound effects (whooshes, pops, cash register dings).

Avoid
- Slow cinematic buildups (viewers will scroll away).
- Vague messaging.
- Forgetting the call to action.
