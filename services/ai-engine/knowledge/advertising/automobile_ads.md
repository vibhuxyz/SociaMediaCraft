# Automobile Advertisement Guide

Goal
Sell the thrill of driving, safety, or status associated with the vehicle.

Target Emotion
- Adrenaline
- Freedom
- Control
- Family Security (for SUVs/Vans)

Typical Story Structure
1. The Road: Establishing a sweeping, majestic landscape or sleek cityscape.
2. The Machine: Close-ups of headlights, grille, or engine start button.
3. The Drive: The car conquering the environment (winding roads, rain, desert).
4. The Interior: Driver's perspective, focusing on luxury, tech, and control.
5. The Destination/Arrival.
6. The Offer/Logo.

Visual Style
- Sleek, reflective surfaces.
- High dynamic range (HDR) look.
- Motion blur on wheels and background to emphasize speed.
- "Magic Hour" lighting.

Camera
- Drone and helicopter sweeping shots.
- Russian arm tracking shots for smooth high-speed car-to-car.
- Wide angle inside the cabin.
- Panning shots to track the car passing.

Music
- Driving rock, aggressive electronic, or soaring orchestral (depending on the car type).
- Engine revs, tire sounds, and gear shifts used rhythmically as percussion.

Avoid
- Static shots of the car for too long.
- Showing traffic or mundane driving conditions.
- Ignoring the sound design of the engine.
