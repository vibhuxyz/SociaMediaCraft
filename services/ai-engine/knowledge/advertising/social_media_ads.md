# Social Media Advertisement Guide

Goal
Stop the scroll, entertain, and deliver the message within 15 seconds.

Target Emotion
- Surprise
- Amusement
- Relatability
- Curiosity

Typical Story Structure
1. The Scroll-Stopper: A visually weird, loud, or highly relatable first 2 seconds.
2. The Context: Fast delivery of the core premise.
3. The Value Prop: Why the viewer should care.
4. The Payoff/Joke: The climax of the short narrative.
5. Native CTA: "Click the link," "Swipe up."

Visual Style
- Authentic, slightly lo-fi (looks native to the platform).
- Dynamic captions on screen.
- Fast-paced, high energy.
- Vertical video (9:16).

Camera
- Selfie-style handheld.
- Quick zooms (The "Office" style snap zooms).
- POV angles.

Music
- Trending audio or fast-paced electronic.
- Memetic sound effects.
- Direct-to-camera speaking (often with lavalier mic audio).

Avoid
- Traditional 16:9 cinematic framing (takes up less screen space).
- Slow establishing shots.
- Corporate jargon.
