# Real Estate Advertisement Guide

Goal
Sell a lifestyle and help the viewer imagine living in the space.

Target Emotion
- Aspiration
- Peace
- Success
- Comfort

Typical Story Structure
1. The Approach: Drone shot flying toward the property.
2. The Welcome: Opening the front door to a grand space.
3. The Flow: Moving through the key living areas (kitchen, living room, master bath).
4. The Details: Close-ups of high-end finishes (marble, appliances).
5. The Lifestyle: The view, the pool, the sunset.
6. Contact Info.

Visual Style
- Bright, airy, and hyper-clean.
- Perfect vertical lines (architectural framing).
- High dynamic range (windows must not be blown out).

Camera
- Wide-angle lenses (14mm-24mm) to make spaces look large but avoid extreme distortion.
- Smooth gimbal walkthroughs.
- Slow pans and tilts on a tripod.

Music
- Upbeat but non-distracting corporate or chillhop.
- Soft piano and acoustic guitar.

Avoid
- Messy rooms or personal clutter.
- Fast, jarring movements.
- Fisheye distortion.
