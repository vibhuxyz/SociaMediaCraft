# Perfume Advertisement Guide

Goal
Sell desire, fantasy, and abstract emotion rather than functional utility.

Target Emotion
- Seduction
- Freedom
- Mystery
- Elegance
- Intimacy

Typical Story Structure
1. Abstract Intro: Mysterious location or subject.
2. The Gaze: Protagonist makes intense eye contact or looks toward a distant goal.
3. The Journey/Encounter: Running, dancing, or meeting a lover.
4. Climax: A moment of liberation, passion, or sensory explosion.
5. Product Reveal: Slow reveal of the bottle.
6. The Whisper: Voiceover saying the brand/perfume name.

Visual Style
- Ethereal, dreamy, or high-contrast filmic look.
- Slow motion (120fps+).
- Elements: Water, silk, wind, petals, gold, city lights.
- Shallow depth of field to isolate the subject.

Camera
- 85mm to 135mm for flattering portraits.
- Slow tracking shots or Steadicam flowing movements.
- Macro for the bottle's glass details.

Music
- Classical strings (e.g., cellos).
- Dramatic ambient synth.
- Breath-heavy or whispering vocals.
- Complete silence abruptly broken by sound.

Avoid
- Explaining the product's ingredients literally.
- Fast, chaotic cuts.
- Comedy or overt humor.
- Too much dialogue.
