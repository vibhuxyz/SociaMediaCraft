# Food Advertisement Guide

Goal
Trigger extreme appetite appeal and sensory cravings.

Target Emotion
- Craving
- Satisfaction
- Comfort
- Excitement

Typical Story Structure
1. The Prep: Fresh ingredients being chopped or falling in slow motion.
2. The Sizzle: Cooking action (grill flames, boiling, frying).
3. The Assembly: Building the dish.
4. The "Pull": The cheese pull, the slow pour of syrup, the steam rising.
5. The Bite: A person reacting with extreme pleasure (optional, sometimes just the food is enough).
6. Brand/Logo.

Visual Style
- Highly saturated colors (especially reds, yellows, and greens).
- Glossy, textured, and steamy.
- Shallow depth of field to force focus on the most appetizing part.

Camera
- Macro lenses (100mm+).
- Slow motion is critical (120fps+).
- Robotic motion control (Bolt) for dynamic liquid pours and ingredient drops.

Music
- Upbeat, rhythmic, sometimes funky.
- Extremely detailed foley (the crunch, the sizzle, the pour, the fizz).

Avoid
- Dull, grey lighting.
- Unappetizing textures (dryness, limp greens).
- Showing the messy aftermath of a meal.
