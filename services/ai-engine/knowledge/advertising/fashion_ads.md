# Fashion Advertisement Guide

Goal
Showcase attitude, silhouette, and movement to define a trend or identity.

Target Emotion
- Confidence
- Edge
- Creativity
- Rebellion or Elegance (depending on brand)

Typical Story Structure
1. The Attitude: Bold opening shot of the model in dynamic pose.
2. The Walk: Rhythmic movement showcasing how the fabric flows.
3. The Details: Quick cuts of accessories, stitching, and textures.
4. The Group/Environment: Interaction with surreal or striking environments.
5. The Stare: Breaking the fourth wall with strong eye contact.
6. Logo Flash.

Visual Style
- High contrast, often mixed with film grain or analog formats (16mm).
- Unconventional color grading (neon, bleach bypass, or black and white).
- Strong geometry and architecture in backgrounds.

Camera
- Wide angles (24mm) for elongated body shapes and dramatic perspective.
- Handheld or rapid snap-zooms for edgy brands; robotic motion-control for high fashion.
- Unconventional angles (low angles, dutch tilts).

Music
- Heavy bass electronic.
- Fast-paced techno or hip-hop.
- Rhythmic, syncopated beats that drive the editing.

Avoid
- Generic, static catalogue poses.
- Boring, flat lighting.
- Explaining the practicality of the clothing.
