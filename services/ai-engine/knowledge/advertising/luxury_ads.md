# Luxury Advertisement Guide

Goal
Communicate exclusivity, heritage, and premium craftsmanship.

Target Emotion
- Status
- Aspiration
- Sophistication
- Confidence
- Timelessness

Typical Story Structure
1. The Environment: Establishing shot of an exclusive location (mansion, yacht, pristine nature).
2. The Detail: Extreme close-up of craftsmanship or material.
3. The Lifestyle: Protagonist moving with absolute confidence.
4. The Moment of Appreciation: Taking time to enjoy the luxury.
5. Subtle Reveal: The brand acts as the signature on the lifestyle.
6. Elegant CTA (often just the logo).

Visual Style
- Rich, deep blacks with gold or silver accents.
- Perfectly clean compositions.
- Symmetrical framing.
- Flawless lighting.

Camera
- Cinematic 35mm or 50mm for environment.
- Macro lenses (100mm) for textures.
- Slow, deliberate dolly moves. No handheld wobble.

Music
- Minimalist piano.
- Low, resonant frequencies.
- Jazz or orchestral arrangements.
- High-quality foley (e.g., the click of a watch clasp, the pour of a drink).

Avoid
- Cluttered backgrounds.
- Shouting or aggressive voiceovers.
- Price mentions.
- Bright, garish colors unless part of a specific brand identity.
