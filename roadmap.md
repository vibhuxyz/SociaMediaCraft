# VideoCraft - Project Roadmap

"The Operating System for Multimodal AI Content Creation"

## Vision
VideoCraft is designed for someone who wants to become an Applied AI Engineer while building a real startup. It focuses on AI orchestration, agent systems, multimodal generation, and production-ready AI.

## Strategic Phases
The project is built across 10 engineering milestones (V0 to V9).
1. **Phase 1: AI Infrastructure & Planning (V0–V2)** - Build the backend queue, LangGraph workflow orchestrator, and planning agents.
2. **Phase 2: AI Intelligence & Media (V3–V6)** - Implement tool calling, MCP, video generation (FFmpeg), audio intelligence, and Memory/RAG.
3. **Phase 3: Production Operations (V7–V9)** - Multimodal understanding, Reflection & Evaluation agents, and production infrastructure (Kubernetes, Kafka).
